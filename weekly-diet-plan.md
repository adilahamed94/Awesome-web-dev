## For the cook (he comes twice daily)

**Morning visit — lunch ready by 10:45, that day's menu above.** Also on gym-day mornings: nothing needed, breakfast dosa/idli batter is batch-made.

**Evening visit — dinner fresh for 10 pm.** Low-oil rule applies hardest here.

**Batters & batch (make Sunday or Monday):**
- **Pesarattu batter:** soak 2 cups whole moong 6 hrs + 2 tbsp rice + green chilli + ginger → grind. Keeps 3–4 days refrigerated.
- **Idli/dosa batter:** standard, or buy fresh batter (iD/Milky Mist on Instamart) — zero effort.
- Sambar: toor dal base, veg from the allowed list (beans, carrot, peas — **no gourds, no brinjal**)

**Standing rules:** no capsicum/brinjal/gourds · olive/mustard/groundnut oil, 3–4 tsp per meal, no ghee/butter · iodized salt · raw salad with lunch and dinner · dinner portions as written — bigger on Tue/Thu/Sat.

## Rules

1. **Whey in milk with breakfast, every day.** It's what holds every day above 100 g protein with only 3–4 meals. Non-negotiable.
2. **Snack days are Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun** and every snack is grab-and-eat: yogurt tub, sprouts box, paneer box, boiled eggs. If even 2 minutes isn't there, whey in water is the rescue.
3. **No-snack days (Tue/Thu/Sat): the bigger dinner is not optional** — 3 cheelas, 5 egg whites, big pulao portion. That's where the missing snack lives.
4. **Dinner low-oil always** — you sleep ~2 hrs later and your LDL (174) is the one number this plan is also trying to fix.
5. Weigh-in Sunday. Flat after 3 weeks → bigger breakfast (extra banana + PB in oats). Gaining >0.5 kg/week → trim lunch rice by ⅓.
6. Creatine stays out per your call. If you ever change your mind, it's the single most-proven safe addition — 3 g daily, nothing else changes.

> ⚠️ Labs, one last time: B12 (167) and Vitamin D (19) need a prescription — food can't fix them fast enough — and mention the TSH (6.11). One doctor visit covers all three, and it will do more for your recovery than any table in this document.
