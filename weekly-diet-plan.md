**Daily:** fresh Indian lunch ready by 10:45 (menu above).
**Evening visit only Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat** for dinner — Tue/Thu/Sun you handle yourself in ≤10 min.

**Standing rules:** no capsicum/brinjal/gourds · olive/mustard/groundnut oil, 3–4 tsp per meal, no ghee/butter · iodized salt · raw salad with lunch and dinner · dinner stays low-oil.

## Sunday 25-minute prep (what makes no-cook mornings work)

1. Overnight oats × 2 jars (Mon + Fri)
2. **Boil 8 eggs** → fridge (breakfasts + Thursday)
3. Cook 1 batch quinoa → fridge (Tuesday's dinner bowl)
4. Soak moong for sprouts (Wed snack); cut paneer cubes into a box (Fri snack)
5. Instamart order: eggs, paneer, Greek yogurt tubs, curd, tofu, avocados, bananas, bread, milk, fruit

## Rules

1. **Whey in milk with breakfast, every day** — it's what holds every day at ~100+ g protein with only 3–4 meals.
2. **Breakfast is assembly, not cooking**: jars, toast, bowls, smoothie, pre-boiled eggs. If a morning collapses, the Saturday smoothie is the 3-minute fallback for any day.
3. **Snack days Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun**, all grab-and-eat. Whey in water is the rescue if even 2 minutes isn't there.
4. **No-snack days (Tue/Thu/Sat): the bigger dinner is not optional** — that's where the missing snack lives.
5. **Dinner low-oil always** — you sleep ~2 hrs later and your LDL (174) is the other number this plan is fixing.
6. Weigh-in Sunday. Flat after 3 weeks → bigger breakfast (extra banana + PB). Gaining >0.5 kg/week → trim lunch rice by ⅓.
7. Creatine stays out per your call; if you change your mind it's 3 g daily and nothing else changes.
