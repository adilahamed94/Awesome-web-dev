# Weekly Diet Plan (Mon–Sun)

**Adil · 66 kg · Muscle gain · Eggitarian · ~2,500–2,600 kcal · 105–120 g protein daily**

## Built around your actual day

- **Before 11:15 am:** pre-workout bite → gym → breakfast → early lunch
- **One snack at ~6 pm, alternate days only** — Mon / Wed / Fri / Sun. That lines up with gym days, when your body most needs the mid-day protein. **On no-snack days (Tue/Thu/Sat), dinner is bigger** to make up for it.
- **~10:00 pm:** dinner — low-oil (you sleep soon after; also better for your LDL)
- **Whey is a daily breakfast item now** (gym or not) — with only 3–4 meals a day, breakfast has to carry 40+ g protein.
- Gym days assumed **Mon / Wed / Fri**; 4th day later = Saturday (it then becomes a snack day too)
- **No capsicum, brinjal, or gourds.** Vegetables: palak, methi, broccoli, cauliflower, green beans, peas, mushrooms, bhindi, cabbage, carrot, beetroot, corn, tomato-onion.

## Rules for this schedule

1. **Whey with breakfast every single day** — with only 3–4 eating occasions, breakfast must carry 40+ g protein. This is the one habit that makes the alternate-day snack pattern work.
2. **Lunch at 10:45 is your biggest meal** — most of the day's rice/roti lives here and at breakfast, not at 10 pm.
3. **No-snack days = bigger dinner.** The plan already reflects this (200 g paneer, 3 cheelas, extra whites). Don't shrink those portions.
4. On no-snack days the lunch→dinner gap is ~11 hours. If hunger gets rough mid-afternoon, whey in water at your desk breaks no rule — it's liquid, 2 minutes, and protects muscle.
5. **Dinner stays low-oil** — you're asleep within ~2 hrs and your LDL (174) doesn't want fried food at 10 pm.
6. Creatine 3–5 g daily with any meal, even rest days.
7. Weigh-in Sunday morning. Flat after 3 weeks → the fix is a bigger breakfast (add 1 banana + 1 tbsp PB to the oats), not more snacks. Gaining >0.5 kg/week → trim rice at lunch by ⅓.

> ⚠️ From your labs: get B12 + D3 prescribed and mention the TSH (6.11) — one doctor visit. These matter more for recovery than anything on this page.
