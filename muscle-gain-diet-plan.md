## 5. Core recipes (make once with the cook, then it's his)

### Quinoa salad (2 servings)
1. Rinse 1 cup quinoa, boil in 2 cups water 15 min until fluffy. Cool.
2. Add: cucumber, tomato, onion, boiled corn, grated carrot, boiled chana or 100 g paneer cubes.
3. Dress: 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, salt, pepper, chaat masala, coriander.
4. Optional: pomegranate + roasted peanuts on top.
*~20 g protein/serving with paneer; keeps 2 days in fridge.*

### Overnight oats (1 jar)
Mason jar → 60 g oats + 1 tbsp chia + 250 ml milk + 1 tbsp peanut butter + ½ banana sliced + pinch cinnamon. Shake, refrigerate overnight. Eat cold or 30 sec in microwave. Add 1 scoop whey to make it 35+ g protein.

### Avocado toast
Halve avocado (buy 2–3 days ahead — they ripen slowly), mash with lemon, salt, chilli flakes. Spread on toasted whole-wheat/sourdough. Top with 2 boiled/poached eggs, pepper. *Lemon stops browning; use the second half next morning, stored with the seed, cling-wrapped.*

### Soy chunk curry (cook's version)
1. Boil 60 g dry soy chunks 5 min in salted water, squeeze out water.
2. Onion-tomato-ginger-garlic masala in 2 tsp oil, add turmeric/chilli/garam masala.
3. Add chunks + ½ cup water (or thin curd gravy), simmer 10 min.
*~30 g protein per serving.*

### Paneer tikka (no tandoor)
Cube 200 g paneer. Marinate 30 min: 3 tbsp thick curd + ginger-garlic paste + kasuri methi + chilli powder + turmeric + salt + 1 tsp oil. Sear on hot tawa/air-fry 12 min at 200°C with onion and mushroom.

### Sprouts (batch, weekly)
Soak moong overnight → drain → keep in covered colander 24–36 h, rinse twice daily. Fridge for the week. Eat raw as bhel or lightly steamed.

## 8. The 8 rules that matter more than the details

1. **Protein at every meal** — 25–30 g each time beats 80 g at dinner.
2. **Surplus, not a see-food diet** — ~300 extra calories. Gaining fat fast helps nothing.
3. **Heart-smart fats only** — your LDL (174) is the one red flag. No ghee/butter/fried while bulking. Recheck lipids in 3 months.
4. **Max 2 whole eggs/day**, unlimited whites.
5. **Fix B12 and D with your doctor** — low levels blunt energy, recovery, and mood; food alone won't correct a 167 B12.
6. **Iron trick**: lemon on everything, no chai/coffee within an hour of meals.
7. **Creatine + whey are the only supplements worth buying.** Everything else is marketing.
8. **Sleep 7–8 h.** Muscle is built in bed, not in the gym.
