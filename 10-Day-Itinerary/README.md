# 🌸 10-Day Stay Itinerary

A relaxed, cozy 10-day plan for when she comes to stay over — days planned out, and every meal mapped to the cook's schedule so nobody has to think about food on the spot.

> **Tweak freely.** Swap any dish, activity, or rest day. Treat this as a starting point, not a rulebook.

---

## 🍳 How the cook works (read this first)

The cook comes **twice a day**:

| Visit | Time (suggested) | What he makes |
|-------|------------------|----------------|
| **Morning** | ~8:00–9:30 AM | **Breakfast** (served hot) **+ prepares Lunch** (kept ready to warm up later) |
| **Evening** | ~6:30–8:00 PM | **Dinner** (served hot) |

**Why lunch is prepped in the morning:** since the cook isn't there at midday, he cooks lunch during the morning visit and leaves it ready. So **lunch dishes are always things that hold well** — curries, dals, rice, roasted veg, cold salads, sandwiches, wraps — nothing that has to be eaten the instant it's made.

### 💬 How she can talk to the cook

She's the one deciding the menu, so here's how to make that easy:

- **The evening before**, glance at the next day's plan below and tell the cook: *"Tomorrow morning, please make ___ for breakfast and ___ for lunch. In the evening, ___ for dinner."*
- **To learn a dish**, she can just ask while he cooks: *"Can you show me how you make this?"* or *"What goes in first?"* Standing in the kitchen for 10 minutes is the best cooking class there is.
- **Ask about spice level**: *"Can we make it a little less/more spicy?"*
- **Ask for substitutions**: *"Can we use paneer instead of chicken?"* / *"No onion today, please."*
- **Portion note**: cooking for 2, so tell him *"just enough for two"* to avoid waste.

There's a printable **daily order card** at the bottom she can fill in each evening.

## Day 1 — Arrival & Settle In 🧳

**Plan:** Pick her up, let her unpack and rest. Keep the day light — no big outings. A slow evening walk, then a warm welcome dinner at home.

- **Breakfast:** Masala omelette + toast + fresh juice *(light, she may arrive on a full stomach)*
- **Lunch:** Jeera rice + dal tadka + a simple salad *(comforting, travel-friendly)*
- **Dinner:** Butter chicken / paneer butter masala + garlic naan + a small dessert (gulab jamun) — a proper welcome meal

---

## Day 2 — Explore the City 🏙️

**Plan:** Head out mid-morning. Walk the main spots, hit a café she'd like, do a bit of shopping. Back by evening.

- **Breakfast:** Poha / upma + filter coffee *(quick, so you can head out)*
- **Lunch:** Veg pulao + raita + papad *(holds well while you're out — warm it up when you're back, or pack it)*
- **Dinner:** Grilled chicken / tandoori paneer + sautéed veggies + roti

---

## Day 3 — Slow & Cozy Day 🎬

**Plan:** Pajamas all day. Movie marathon, snacks, long conversations. No pressure to do anything.

- **Breakfast:** Aloo paratha + curd + pickle *(the ultimate cozy breakfast)*
- **Lunch:** Rajma + rice *(classic comfort, holds beautifully)*
- **Dinner:** Homemade pizza night OR pav bhaji — something fun and hands-on

---

## Day 4 — Adventure Day 🥾

**Plan:** Day trip — a viewpoint, lake, trail, or a nearby town. Leave early. Ask the cook to keep lunch packable if you'll be out.

- **Breakfast:** Idli / dosa + sambar + chutney *(hearty fuel for the day)*
- **Lunch:** Lemon rice / curd rice + a dry sabzi — **ask the cook to pack it as a picnic** if you're out
- **Dinner:** Something warm and easy after a long day — khichdi + ghee + papad, or a light soup + grilled sandwich

---

## Day 5 — Pamper & Self-Care Day 💆

**Plan:** Spa-at-home. Face masks, music, a long bath, nap. Zero errands.

- **Breakfast:** Fruit bowl + yogurt + granola / smoothie *(fresh and light)*
- **Lunch:** Grilled veg + hummus + pita, or a big salad bowl *(clean, refreshing)*
- **Dinner:** Comfort food she loves — pasta in a creamy/red sauce + garlic bread

---

## Day 6 — Cook Together 👩‍🍳

**Plan:** The fun one. She picks a dish she's always wanted to learn, and the cook teaches her step by step during the morning visit. She does the hands-on part; he guides.

- **Breakfast:** Keep it simple — she'll be busy cooking. Bread-butter-jam + eggs + coffee
- **Lunch:** **Her project** — whatever she chooses to learn (biryani, a curry, a dessert). The cook assists.
- **Dinner:** Leftovers from her cooking + one fresh dish from the cook, so nothing goes to waste

> 💡 Ask the cook the evening before: *"Tomorrow, can you teach her to make ___? Please bring the ingredients."*

---

## Day 7 — Culture Day 🎨

**Plan:** Museum, art gallery, old town, or a big local market. Slow wandering, street snacks, photos.

- **Breakfast:** Chole bhature OR a big English breakfast *(so you can skip a heavy lunch and snack outside)*
- **Lunch:** Light — vegetable soup + sandwich, or just a wrap the cook preps *(you'll be snacking out)*
- **Dinner:** A regional cuisine she likes — South Indian thali, or a Chinese-style stir-fry + fried rice

---

## Day 8 — Lazy Brunch & Games 🎲

**Plan:** Sleep in. Late, big brunch. Board games / cards / video games in the afternoon. Nowhere to be.

- **Breakfast (late brunch):** The big one — pancakes / French toast + eggs + hash browns + fresh juice. Tell the cook to come a bit later and make it generous.
- **Lunch:** Skip or very light (you'll be full from brunch) — a fruit plate or salad the cook leaves out
- **Dinner:** BBQ / grill vibe — tikkas, grilled corn, sautéed veg + garlic rice

---

## Day 9 — Special Date Night 🌹

**Plan:** The centerpiece. Dress up. Maybe go out for the afternoon (flowers, a nice walk), then come home to a candlelit dinner the cook has made special.

- **Breakfast:** Something sweet — waffles / stuffed toast + coffee
- **Lunch:** Keep it light so dinner is the star — a fresh salad + soup
- **Dinner:** **The special one.** Ask the cook a day ahead to go all out: a multi-course dinner — starter, a rich main (her favorite), and a proper dessert. Candles, good music, the works.

> 💡 Tell the cook 2 days ahead so he can shop for anything special.

---

## Day 10 — Wind Down & Goodbye 🕊️

**Plan:** No rushing. A slow, warm morning together. A relaxed last meal. Send her off feeling cared for, not tired.

- **Breakfast:** Her favorite breakfast from the trip — let her pick, one more time
- **Lunch:** A gentle, home-y meal — dal, rice, her favorite sabzi *(comforting for a travel day)*
- **Dinner:** *(If she leaves in the evening, pack her something for the road — a few parathas + sabzi wrapped up.)*

---

## 🛒 Shopping / Prep Notes for the Cook

Give the cook a heads-up on these so he can stock up:

- **Staples (whole stay):** rice, atta/flour, dals, onions, tomatoes, ginger-garlic, oil/ghee, spices, eggs, milk, curd, bread, tea/coffee
- **Proteins:** rotate chicken / paneer / fish as she prefers
- **Fresh daily-ish:** vegetables, fruit, salad greens, lemons, coriander
- **Special days:** Day 6 (whatever she chooses to learn), Day 9 (special dinner + dessert ingredients — tell him 2 days early)
