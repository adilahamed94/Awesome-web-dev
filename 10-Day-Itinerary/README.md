# 💪 10-Day Healthy Routine — Stay Itinerary

A 10-day plan built around a **consistent daily routine**: early-morning park walk, gym, healthy home-cooked meals (chicken, salads, smoothies — taught by the cook), and pickleball on some evenings. Same rhythm every day so it actually becomes a habit, with the details varied so it never gets boring.

> **Tweak freely.** Shift the times to fit your sleep, swap any dish or workout. The routine is the point — keep the skeleton, change the filling.

---

## ⏰ The Daily Routine (the skeleton — same every day)

| Time | What |
|------|------|
| **6:15 AM** | Wake up, water + light stretch |
| **6:30 AM** | 🌳 **Walk in the park together** (30–40 min brisk walk) |
| **7:15 AM** | 💪 **Gym** (see weekly split below) |
| **8:30 AM** | Home, shower |
| **8:45 AM** | 🍳 **Cook's morning visit** — makes **breakfast + smoothie**, preps **lunch**; she learns a dish |
| **9:15 AM** | Breakfast + smoothie together |
| **1:00 PM** | Lunch (prepped in the morning — just warm/assemble) |
| **4:00 PM** | Light snack (fruit / nuts) |
| **6:30 PM** | 🏓 **Pickleball** (Days 2, 4, 6, 8, 10) OR relaxed evening (other days) |
| **7:45 PM** | 🍽️ **Cook's evening visit** — makes **dinner**; she learns the protein/salad |
| **8:15 PM** | Dinner together |
| **10:30 PM** | Wind down, lights out |

### 🍳 How the cook fits the routine

He comes **twice a day**:
- **Morning (~8:45 AM):** makes **breakfast + a fresh smoothie**, and **preps lunch** to leave ready (he's not there at midday, so lunch is always something that holds — grilled chicken, salads, cooked grains).
- **Evening (~7:45 PM):** makes **dinner** fresh.

Because lunch is prepped in the morning, **lunches here are salads, grain bowls, and grilled chicken** — healthy things that keep well for a few hours.

### 💬 How she learns to cook (the daily ask)

She's driving the menu and learning as she goes. Each visit, she stands in the kitchen 10–15 min and does the hands-on part while the cook guides:

- *"Can you show me how you grill the chicken today? What's in the marinade?"*
- *"Teach me to build this salad — what's the dressing?"*
- *"How do you make this smoothie? What makes it filling but not heavy?"*
- Healthy asks: *"Less oil today,"* *"grilled not fried,"* *"more protein,"* *"no added sugar in the smoothie."*

The goal by Day 10: **she can make a healthy chicken dish, a good salad, and a smoothie on her own.** The plan below builds that up gradually.

## 🥗 The Cooking Progression (chicken · salad · smoothie)

| Day | 🐔 Chicken (learn) | 🥗 Salad (learn) | 🥤 Smoothie (learn) |

## 🗓️ Day-by-Day (routine + the day's meals)

Each day follows the **routine above**. Below is what's different: the meals and the evening.

### Day 1 — Ease In 🌱
- **Breakfast:** Veggie omelette + toast · **Smoothie:** banana + peanut butter
- **Lunch:** Grilled chicken breast + green salad + brown rice
- **Evening:** Relaxed — settle in, easy walk
- **Dinner:** Grilled fish/chicken + sautéed veg + salad

### Day 2 — First Pickleball 🏓
- **Breakfast:** Oats + fruit · **Smoothie:** berry-yogurt
- **Lunch:** Lemon-herb chicken + cucumber-tomato salad
- **Evening:** 🏓 **Pickleball** (6:30 PM)
- **Dinner:** Chicken + quinoa + steamed greens

### Day 3 — Cozy but On-Routine 🍵
- **Breakfast:** Egg bhurji + multigrain toast · **Smoothie:** mango-spinach green
- **Lunch:** Tandoori-style chicken + chickpea salad
- **Evening:** Rest / stretch / movie
- **Dinner:** Grilled chicken + big mixed salad

### Day 4 — Active Recovery + Pickleball 🏓
- **Breakfast:** Poha / upma + fruit · **Smoothie:** oats-banana (post-workout)
- **Lunch:** Chicken tikka skewers + Greek salad
- **Evening:** 🏓 **Pickleball** (6:30 PM)
- **Dinner:** Light — grilled chicken lettuce wraps + soup

### Day 5 — Strong Middle 💪
- **Breakfast:** Scrambled eggs + avocado toast · **Smoothie:** avocado-banana
- **Lunch:** Pan-seared chicken + quinoa veg bowl
- **Evening:** Relaxed — self-care evening
- **Dinner:** Grilled chicken + roasted veg + salad

### Day 6 — Cook Day + Pickleball 🏓👩‍🍳
- **Breakfast:** Her-made smoothie + eggs (she leads breakfast today)
- **Lunch:** Grilled chicken + homemade salsa + Mediterranean salad (she cooks)
- **Evening:** 🏓 **Pickleball** (6:30 PM)
- **Dinner:** Leftovers from her cooking + one fresh dish

### Day 7 — Reset & Refuel 🥗
- **Breakfast:** Chilla / besan pancake + fruit · **Smoothie:** beetroot-apple-ginger
- **Lunch:** Chicken shawarma-style + rocket-orange salad
- **Evening:** Rest / walk / farmers' market for fresh produce
- **Dinner:** Grilled chicken + big salad bowl

### Day 8 — Pickleball Night 🏓
- **Breakfast:** Overnight oats · **Smoothie:** chocolate protein
- **Lunch:** Chicken stir-fry + Asian slaw
- **Evening:** 🏓 **Pickleball** (6:30 PM)
- **Dinner:** Light stir-fry veg + grilled chicken

### Day 9 — She Leads the Kitchen 👩‍🍳
- **Breakfast:** Her choice, she makes it · **Smoothie:** her choice
- **Lunch:** Her choice — chicken + salad, cook only assists
- **Evening:** Relaxed / dress-up healthy dinner at home
- **Dinner:** A nice-but-healthy special — grilled chicken, roasted veg, a light dessert (fruit + yogurt)

### Day 10 — Solo Test + Send-Off 🏓🕊️
- **Breakfast:** She makes the whole thing solo (eggs + smoothie) — cook just watches
- **Lunch:** She makes chicken + salad solo — final "graduation"
- **Evening:** 🏓 **Pickleball** (last one!) or a slow walk
- **Dinner:** Her favorite healthy meal from the 10 days, one more time

## 🛒 Shopping / Prep Notes for the Cook

Ask him to keep these stocked:

- **Protein:** chicken breast (main), eggs, Greek yogurt, fish (optional), tofu/paneer (variety)
- **Smoothie base:** bananas, mixed berries, mango, spinach, oats, peanut butter, milk / yogurt, honey, cocoa, avocado, beetroot, ginger
- **Salad:** greens, cucumber, tomato, onion, bell peppers, chickpeas, quinoa, feta, olives, nuts/seeds, lemons, olive oil
- **Grains/carbs:** brown rice, quinoa, oats, multigrain bread
- **Keep low:** deep-fried food, added sugar, heavy cream — this is a clean-eating stretch
